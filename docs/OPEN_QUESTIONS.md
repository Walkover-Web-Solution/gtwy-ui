# Open Questions

Questions the repository does not answer. An owner decides each one; once answered, record the answer in [VISION.md](./VISION.md) and remove it here.

## Product vision

1. **Primary persona.** Who is the primary customer: role, workflow, technical level? For example, developers integrating AI into their product, no-code builders, or business teams.
2. **Auditors / reviewers.** Are auditors or reviewers a target persona, and what do they need beyond chat history, metrics and alerts?
3. **Differentiators.** What sets GTWY apart from other AI gateway and agent platforms for each problem in the Problems Solved table?
4. **Strategic priorities.** What are the current top priorities, and how do the surfaces rank (dashboard, agent embed, RAG embed, API, coding-agent skill)?
5. **Success metrics.** Which metrics define success (for example, activation, agents published, embed adoption, retention, revenue), and what are the targets?
6. **Non-goals.** What does the product deliberately not do? For example: is it in scope to host or fine-tune models, to build general workflow automation, or to serve end users directly outside embeds?
7. **"Connect 5000+ apps in just 1 click".** Is this site-title claim current product direction, and which integration (tools, MCP, a specific provider) does it refer to?
8. **Beta features.** Are Widgets and the `orchestratal_model` feature (Beta badge in `components/sliders/MainSlider.js`, not in current navigation) approved direction, experiments, or retired?
9. **Monetization.** Are the Free / Pro plans, credit packs, lifetime access and refer-and-earn part of the long-term direction, and does pricing constrain features in the frontend?
10. **Coding-agent skill source.** `utils/gtwySkill.js` names `https://gtwy.ai/docs/agent-quickstart.md` as the skill's single source of truth, but that file is not in this repository. Where is it maintained, and who owns it?
